    How do I get my business mentioned by ChatGPT?

    Allow OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt and put the answer in the HTML. Those are the two levers OpenAI documents. Everything else being sold is inference.AI search

    How do I know if the LLMs trust my service?

    You cannot know directly. No vendor publishes trust or ranking factors for AI answers. What you can do is observe what they say about you, and test it.AI search

    How do I show up in AI search and AI Overviews?

    For Google, its own documentation says there is nothing special to do. Be indexed, be snippet eligible, rank. For ChatGPT and Perplexity the levers are different.AI search

  14. 14

    How is AEO different from SEO?

    SEO optimises for a ranked list of links. AEO optimises for being the source a model quotes when it answers directly. The crawlers do not all work the same way.AI search
