One engagement. Six parts.

What do you cost?
Audit and strategy01

Know which fix to make first

For when you can't tell which fix to make first. We review the main pages, search visibility, enquiry flow and your data with marketing and sales, agree the business goal and record a baseline. I rank the work by impact, evidence and effort, and I own the roadmap.

Website design and development02

Up-to-date pages that lead to an enquiry

For a site that's out of date and doesn't bring in enquiries. We improve the agreed pages' design, structure, speed and mobile experience, with a clear route to an enquiry. We test every change and keep the components and editing rules your team works with. Nothing goes live without your OK.

SEO and AI search03

Easier to find in Google and in AI answers

For a site that's hard to find in Google or AI answers. We find the questions your buyers ask, fix what stops search engines and AI tools reading the site, tighten structure and internal links, and fill the content gaps we agree on. Search visits and AI mentions are reported separately.

Lead capture and handoff04

Every enquiry reaches a named owner in sales

For forms that don't fit how your sales team works. We connect one main form to your sales system, with a named lead owner and an approved reply, and test it end to end. If it breaks, an alert goes to a named person. Your sales team owns follow-up. Wider automation is quoted separately.

Measurement and a monthly report05

See what the site brings in each month

For a team that can't see what the site brings in beyond GA4. We agree what counts as an enquiry sales accepts. Each month I report search visits, enquiries and handoffs against the baseline. AI mentions stay apart from the hard numbers, and if data is missing, we'll tell you.

Monthly strategy and delivery06

A site that stays current for buyers, search engines and AI tools

A site has to stay current for buyers, search engines and AI tools. We meet weekly, share a monthly plan and work on one priority at a time. Agents and freelancers do the production. I review everything before it ships.

Outside the scope

01Paid ads
02Replacing your sales system

Paid ads and replacing your sales system. SEO and AI search share the time we agree for the rest of the work. Nobody can guarantee rankings or AI citations, including us.
