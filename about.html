Who’s behind Yar & Co?

I'm Yar Al Roshidi. I lead Yar & Co, a small website studio in Dubai for B2B marketing teams in tech companies. We build the site, then stay on to improve how buyers find it and what it brings in.

ID · Dubai · --:--

Yar Al RoshidiFounder, Yar & Co

Based in Dubai, working with companies in the US, the UAE and worldwide.

01

What I do

I take responsibility for the website from the marketing side: deciding what it needs, designing and building it, connecting its forms to sales and reporting what it brings in each month. Search and AI search are part of that work, so that buyers who ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google for a shortlist land on your pages.

02

How the studio works

I lead every project and I’m your contact from the first call. AI agents I trained handle research, early design and motion work, secure-code review and content ideas. Freelance developers, video editors and motion designers join when a project needs them. I review everything before it goes to you. Who does what.

03

Tools

Webflow is our main platform and I’m a Webflow Certified Partner. Zapier and Make connect forms to sales tools. Claude and Lovable handle a lot of the production, and I review what they make.

04

Credentials

Webflow Certified Partner and a member of Webflow's Advisory Council of Experts. See every credential and where it comes from.

05

The short version

Before Yar & Co, I was senior Webflow partner at Noco in Amsterdam, leading development on large builds for EU and US brands. NUMI (YC) and Amazon ran alongside. The full timeline, and the one-page CV.
